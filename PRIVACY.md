# babylatte – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

babylatte helps parents find pram- and baby-friendly cafés. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

**Publisher and data controller:** SAINTS Consultancy, {{ADDRESS}}, Denmark (CVR {{CVR}}). Contact: {{EMAIL}}

## What stays on your device
- **Your location.** If you allow location access, the app uses it only to centre the map and sort cafés by distance. Your GPS position is never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Saved cafés, filters and last map position.** Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
- **Offline café data.** A copy of public OpenStreetMap café data, refreshed daily.
- **Your reports.** A list of the reports you sent (café name, date, note number), so you can follow their status.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this. We have no server and never receive a copy.

## What is sent to third parties
The app talks directly to these public services:

| Service | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GitHub Pages (raghavian.github.io) | A request for the offline café data file, about once a day | To keep the café data on your phone up to date |
| OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org) | The map area you are viewing (as map-tile requests) | To draw the map |
| Overpass API (overpass-api.de and a backup mirror) | The rectangle of the map you search, only for areas not covered by the offline data | To find cafés in that area |
| Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) | The text you type in the search box, when no café name matches | To find the city or neighbourhood |
| OpenStreetMap API (api.openstreetmap.org) | When you send a report: the café's location, your answers and comment. Afterwards: the note numbers of your reports | To publish your report as a public note, and to show you whether it has been resolved |

Like any web server, these services can see your IP address. Their privacy policies apply: [OpenStreetMap Foundation](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy) and [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Reports are public
Reports are posted **anonymously and publicly** as OpenStreetMap notes under the Open Database License. Don't put personal information in your comments. Notes can be closed by OpenStreetMap volunteers, but they stay visible in the public history.

## Your rights
We don't collect or store personal data about you, so there's nothing for us to give you, correct or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your phone, and uninstalling removes it. For notes you posted, OpenStreetMap's own processes apply. Questions or complaints: {{EMAIL}}. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

## Children
babylatte is made for parents, not for children, and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.
