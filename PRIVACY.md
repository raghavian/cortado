# Cortado – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Cortado helps parents find pram- and baby-friendly cafés. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your device
- **Your location.** If you allow location access, the app uses it only to centre the map and sort cafés by distance. Your GPS position is never sent to us or anyone else.
- **Saved cafés, filters and last map position.** These are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## What is sent to third parties
The app has no server of its own. It downloads public café data from GitHub Pages and talks directly to OpenStreetMap services:

| Service | What is sent | Why |
|---|---|---|
| OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org) | The map area you are viewing (as map-tile requests) and your IP address | To draw the map |
| GitHub Pages (raghavian.github.io) | A request for the offline café data file, about once a day | To keep the café data on your phone up to date |
| Overpass API (overpass-api.de and a backup mirror) | The rectangle of the map you search, only for areas not covered by the offline data | To find cafés in that area |
| Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) | The text you type in the search box | To find the city or neighbourhood |
| OpenStreetMap API (api.openstreetmap.org) | Only when you send a report: the café's location, your answers and comment | To publish your report as a public OpenStreetMap note |

Like any web server, these services can see your IP address. Their privacy policies apply: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy and https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Reports are public
Reports are posted **anonymously and publicly** as OpenStreetMap notes under the Open Database License. Please don't put personal information in your comments. Notes can be closed by OpenStreetMap volunteers, but they stay visible in the public history.

## Children
Cortado is made for parents, not for children, and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.
