# Google Play store listing (draft)

**App name (30 chars max):** Cortado: Baby-Friendly Cafés

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Find pram-friendly cafés with changing tables, highchairs & step-free access.

**Full description:**
Out with the pram and need a coffee? Cortado shows you which cafés near you are ready for little ones.

🍼 Filter by what matters
• Baby friendly
• Changing table
• Pram / step-free access (ramps, no steps)
• Highchairs
• Play corner
• Breastfeeding friendly
• Accessible toilet, toilets and outdoor seating

🗺️ Works in any city
Search Copenhagen, London, Berlin or anywhere else. Green pins are baby friendly, amber pins have pram access, and grey pins don't have any info yet.

❤️ Save your favourites
Keep a list of your go-to spots, saved on your phone.

🤝 Made better by parents
Been somewhere with your baby? Tap "Add or correct info" and share what you found. Reports go to OpenStreetMap, the free map of the world, so they help every parent and every app.

🔒 Private by design
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your location never leaves your phone.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

## Data safety form answers
- Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes**: approximate/precise location is *not* collected (used on the device only). The only data that leaves the device is what the user types into a report, which is published on OpenStreetMap.
  - Declare: **"App activity → Other user-generated content"** is *shared* (with OpenStreetMap), *not collected* by you, user-initiated and optional.
  - Search queries go straight to Nominatim. Under Play's definitions, data sent to third-party services counts as "shared", so declare **"App activity → In-app search history"** as shared, optional and not stored by you.
- Is data encrypted in transit? **Yes** (all HTTPS).
- Can users request deletion? No account exists; local data is deleted on uninstall.
